Frame interpolation (FrameMode=1): the method - keeping the game's 30 Hz logic,
recording each frame's Direct3D calls and replaying them with the vertex-shader
constants blended between two frames, with a jump threshold for camera cuts - is
the idea of sh4vita by vallfrr (https://github.com/vallfrr/sh4vita). No sh4vita
source code is included: the constant pairing was written clean-room from a
functional description, and the rest is our own Direct3D 8 code.

The hash used by the frame interpolation follows the public-domain MurmurHash3
(Austin Appleby) mixing steps.

Button Config pad glyphs (winmm.dll v3.11.0, "[Input] PadGlyphs"): the Xbox,
PlayStation and Nintendo button glyphs are from "Input Prompts" 1.5A by Kenney
(www.kenney.nl), released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0,
http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Credit is not required by
the licence and is given with thanks. The glyphs were reduced to 32 x 32 texels
and DXT3-encoded by tools/gen_glyphs.py; no Konami or console-maker artwork is
included (the game's own icon atlas is not copied: the glyphs are written into
its empty half at run time).
